About Trubics

Trubics challenges you to navigate through mysterious landscapes filled with hidden dangers. Think ahead, calculate your moves, and make your way to victory. Perfect for puzzle lovers and strategy enthusiasts!

Download for Android Download for Apple

How to Play

Your mission is simple: safely reach the finish cube without stepping on hidden traps. Think every move through!

Each number tells you how many traps are hiding nearby. Use this vital clue to plan your safest route.

Hold down on a cube to place a flag, marking a suspected trap. You won’t be able to step on flagged tiles.

Hit the Overview button to reveal the entire map layout. Strategize your path with a full view of the battlefield!

Need a lifeline? Use the Hint button to uncover the location of one hidden trap and save yourself from danger!
